## The End of an Era

**The story of Os Caras (The Guys) ends here.**

After competing in OBR 2026, the team is bringing this chapter to a close. Despite securing **2nd place at the regional stage** and qualifying for the state stage, our performance at the state competition fell significantly short of our expectations, and we fell well short of qualifying for the national stage.

Our robot was essentially the same machine that had competed successfully at the regional stage, even featuring additional structural reinforcements. However, during the state competition, it experienced recurring technical failures that severely compromised its performance. Despite repeated attempts to troubleshoot the issues by replacing the EV3 brick, sensors, and motors, we were unable to achieve the reliability needed to compete as intended.

These technical difficulties were compounded by organizational challenges, limited development time, and inadequate resources and testing conditions for proper development and refinement. Together, these factors affected our preparation and ultimately contributed to our disappointing result at the state stage.

However, this is not the end of our journey in robotics. As we move forward under a new name, we intend to explore new challenges by transitioning to the **Rescue Maze** category. At the same time, we hope to welcome new members and share the experience we have gained throughout our time competing in the Rescue Line category, helping the next generation of competitors build upon what we have learned.

Although the future structure of the team has yet to be decided, our goal remains the same: to keep learning, developing, and pushing our engineering skills further.

This repository will remain as a record of our work, development process, achievements, and lessons learned throughout OBR 2026.

**Os Caras may be coming to an end, but the experience gained will carry forward into whatever comes next.**
